The Journey

My journey into design started 8 years ago as a self-taught enthusiast. I’ve always been fascinated by how design can solve complex human problems and create intuitive digital experiences.

After years of dedicated practice, I transitioned into a professional role at Nithra Apps, where I lead end-to-end UX design for mobile and web applications, from user research and usability testing to high-fidelity product design and scalable design systems.

The Philosophy

I believe the best designs are the ones that feel simple, trustworthy, and effortless. My goal is to craft interfaces that are not just beautiful, but deeply functional, inclusive, and driven by real user needs.
